The Three Doshas

Three energies. One balance.

Ayurveda describes the body through three fundamental energies. Understanding them is the first step toward understanding yourself.

🌬️

Vata

Associated with movement, creativity, lightness and the elements of air and space.

🔥

Pitta

Associated with transformation, digestion, metabolism and the elements of fire and water.

🌊

Kapha

Associated with stability, structure, nourishment and the elements of earth and water.
